常见问题

晚安守护会读取聊天内容或网页内容吗？

不会。晚安守护仅基于你在系统 Screen Time 选择器中主动选择的 App、类别或网站范围执行限制，不读取其它 App 的聊天内容、网页正文、照片、通讯录或精确位置。

规则为什么没有生效？

请确认已授予 Screen Time 权限、规则处于开启状态、当前时间落在规则时间段内，并且已经选择限制范围。限制执行也可能受 iOS 版本、系统权限、家庭共享设置或 Apple 系统策略影响。

Pro 内购包含什么？

Pro 可解锁无限规则、iCloud 配置和管理密码。当前提供周会员、季度会员、年会员自动续订订阅，以及终身会员非消耗型项目。实际价格、币种、免费试用资格和订阅状态以 App Store / StoreKit 展示为准。

如何恢复购买或取消订阅？

你可以在 App 的 Pro 页面点击“恢复购买”。自动续订订阅的取消、退款、账单和支付方式问题由 Apple 处理，请在 iOS 设置或 App Store 的 Apple ID 订阅与购买记录中管理。

本地数据如何删除？

你可以在 App 的“我的”页面清空本机数据，也可以卸载 App 或通过系统设置清除 App 数据。如果你使用 iCloud 配置同步，还需要通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 设置管理相关云端数据。

Common Questions

Does NightWard read chat or website content?

No. NightWard applies restrictions only to the apps, categories, or websites you choose through the system Screen Time picker. It does not read chat content, webpage bodies, photos, contacts, or precise location.

Why did a rule not take effect?

Please confirm that Screen Time permission has been granted, the rule is enabled, the current time is within the rule schedule, and a restriction scope has been selected. Restriction behavior may also be affected by iOS version, system permissions, Family Sharing settings, or Apple system policies.

What does Pro include?

Pro unlocks unlimited rules, iCloud configuration, and admin PIN features. Current Pro products include weekly, quarterly, and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions, plus a lifetime non-consumable purchase. Actual prices, currencies, free trial eligibility, and subscription status are shown by the App Store / StoreKit.

How can I restore purchases or cancel a subscription?

You can tap Restore Purchases on the app's Pro page. Auto-renewable subscription cancellation, refunds, billing, and payment method issues are handled by Apple. Manage them in iOS Settings or App Store Apple ID subscription and purchase history screens.

How can I delete local data?

You can clear local data from the app's Mine page, uninstall the app, or clear app data through system settings. If you use iCloud configuration sync, you may also need to manage related cloud data through iCloud settings provided by Apple.

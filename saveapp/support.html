Common Questions

Where is my saved app data stored?

AppPin stores your favorites, folders, notes, and recent views locally on your device by default. If you choose to use iCloud sync, related data may be synced through Apple iCloud.

Can AppPin delete or uninstall apps from my iPhone?

No. AppPin is an app discovery, saving, organization, and tracking tool. It does not automatically delete, uninstall, hide, or manage other apps on your device.

Why are some App Store details different by region?

App information, prices, availability, rankings, and ratings may vary by country or region because the data comes from Apple App Store services.

AppPin Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 27, 2026

AppPin is an App Store app bookmarking and lightweight management tool. We do not provide our own account system, do not operate a developer-hosted server for storing your app library, do not integrate ad tracking SDKs, and do not sell your personal information.

1. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide app search, saved apps, folders, notes, downloaded markers, recent views, price tracking, and version tracking, AppPin may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Saved app information, such as app name, App Store ID, developer, icon, category, price, rating, version, and App Store link.
  • Folders, notes, downloaded markers, tracking preferences, price or version change records, recent views, and local library snapshots you create.
  • Your selected App Store country or region, interface language, iCloud sync status, import/export status, and other local preferences.
  • App Store links or text you share into AppPin through the system share sheet, used to identify and save the corresponding app.

2. App Store, iCloud, and Apple Services

AppPin uses Apple's public iTunes Search API, Lookup API, and Apple Marketing Tools RSS data to search apps, show charts, display app details, and refresh price or version information for apps you save. This public app information is provided by Apple or app developers.

If you choose to upload or download iCloud data, your app library snapshot may be stored through Apple iCloud Documents / CloudKit in the iCloud space associated with your Apple ID. Apple provides the storage, sync, security, and deletion mechanisms for iCloud.

AppPin does not automatically uninstall other apps, does not read chat content, webpage bodies, photos, contacts, precise location, or personal files inside other apps, and does not bypass iOS permissions to access your device content.

3. Accounts, Advertising, and Payments

AppPin currently does not require you to register an account or sign in to a developer server. We do not integrate advertising SDKs, conduct cross-app advertising tracking, or sell your personal information.

The current version does not process payment card information or user account information through a developer-operated server. If we add in-app purchases, subscriptions, or account features in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and App Store privacy disclosures before those features are released.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Libraries

The current version uses the following third-party SDKs or software libraries:

  • Tencent Bugly SDK: used to detect and fix crashes and improve app stability. Bugly may collect diagnostic information when the app crashes or encounters an exception, such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, exception stack traces, network status, and device identifiers generated by Bugly for stability analysis. We do not use Bugly for advertising, ad tracking, or user profiling. Bugly's handling of this information is described in the Bugly SDK Privacy Statement.
  • Alamofire: used to implement network requests. Alamofire does not collect, transmit, or store personal information as an independent third-party service.
  • SnapKit: used to build app interface layouts. SnapKit does not collect, transmit, or store personal information as an independent third-party service.
  • LookinServer: used only in Debug development builds for interface inspection. It is not used in the App Store release version for data collection or user tracking.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your name, phone number, government ID, contacts, or precise location.
  • We do not integrate advertising SDKs, conduct cross-app advertising tracking, or sell your personal information.
  • We do not read the specific content of other apps, and we do not record chat content, webpage bodies, photos, media files, or personal files.
  • Except for Bugly crash diagnostics, we do not currently use third-party analytics SDKs to track your in-app behavior.

6. How We Use Data

We process the local information described above only to provide features you choose to use, including searching apps, saving apps, organizing folders, recording notes, showing recent views, refreshing price or version changes, saving apps from the share sheet, importing or exporting your library, performing iCloud sync that you initiate, and locating and fixing crashes.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Permission Withdrawal

Local data remains on your device until you clear local data in the app, uninstall the app, or clear app data through system settings. If you use iCloud sync, related snapshots may remain in your iCloud space, and you can manage them through iCloud settings or other methods provided by Apple.

You can stop using iCloud sync, delete local data, or uninstall the app. Withdrawing network access may affect app search, charts, detail refresh, and price or version tracking features that rely on public App Store data.

8. Children

AppPin provides app bookmarking and organization features for general users. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Parents or guardians should decide whether children may use the app based on applicable laws, Apple App Store rules, and family device management requirements.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in features, legal requirements, third-party SDKs, iCloud capabilities, or App Store review requirements. When we update the policy, we will revise the effective date on this page. For material changes, we will try to provide notice in the app, on the App Store page, or on this page.

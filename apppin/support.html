常见问题

AppPin 会读取聊天内容或网页内容吗？

不会。AppPin 只保存你主动收藏的 App 信息、备注、文件夹和最近浏览等内容，不读取其它 App 的聊天内容、网页正文、照片、通讯录或精确位置。

为什么搜索或详情没有刷新？

请确认网络连接可用，并尝试切换 App Store 地区或稍后重试。搜索、榜单、价格和版本信息来自 Apple 公开接口，可能受网络、地区、App 下架或 Apple 服务状态影响。

AppPin 会自动卸载 App 或清理手机空间吗？

不会。AppPin 是收藏和整理工具，不会自动卸载 App、删除文件或修改系统存储。你可以先保存 App 信息，再自行通过 iOS 卸载低频 App。

如何使用 iCloud 同步？

你可以在 App 的“我的”页面主动上传或下载收藏库快照。下载会用 iCloud 中的快照覆盖当前本地收藏数据，操作前请确认本机数据已经妥善处理。

本地数据如何删除？

你可以在 App 的“我的”页面清空本机数据，也可以卸载 App 或通过系统设置清除 App 数据。如果你使用 iCloud 配置同步，还需要通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 设置管理相关云端数据。

Common Questions

Does AppPin read chat or website content?

No. AppPin only stores app information, notes, folders, and recent views that you choose to save. It does not read chat content, webpage bodies, photos, contacts, or precise location inside other apps.

Why did search or app details not refresh?

Please check your network connection, try another App Store country or region, or try again later. Search, charts, prices, and version information come from Apple's public services and may be affected by network conditions, region availability, app removal, or Apple service status.

Does AppPin automatically uninstall apps or clean phone storage?

No. AppPin is a bookmarking and organization tool. It does not automatically uninstall apps, delete files, or modify system storage. You can save app information first, then uninstall low-use apps yourself through iOS.

How can I use iCloud sync?

You can manually upload or download your app library snapshot from the Profile page. Downloading replaces the current local saved data with the snapshot in iCloud, so please confirm your local data before continuing.

How can I delete local data?

You can clear local data from the app's Mine page, uninstall the app, or clear app data through system settings. If you use iCloud configuration sync, you may also need to manage related cloud data through iCloud settings provided by Apple.
